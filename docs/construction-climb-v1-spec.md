# Construction Climb v1

## Reference direction
Construction Climb is an original Moving Screen map inspired by the uploaded construction-site traversal video: broad yard, temporary ramps and scaffolds, unfinished concrete floors, exposed rebar, stacked materials, and a dangerous upper construction deck. It should preserve the readable physical logic of the reference without reproducing its exact layout or art.

## Mode rules
- Moving Screen rules remain unchanged: 10 lives, 30 KOs, crown flag, four border death planes, direct summoning, manual crossroads, route tap/swipe, destructible topology, canonical results.
- Direction is upward.
- Early camera bands are intentionally forgiving with 2-3 viable ascent routes.
- Chokes become more consequential in the upper half.

## Structural progression
1. Ground Work Yard
2. Scaffold Interior
3. Unfinished Mid Floors
4. Crane / Suspended Beam Crossing
5. Upper Slabs
6. Rooftop Crane Crown

## Physical readability
- Standable combat zones are concrete slabs, shipping-container roofs, lumber/plywood stacks, scaffold decks, stair landings, temporary ramps, utility platforms, and steel beams.
- Physical connectors should explain routes before the white guide line does: plank ramps, stairs, scaffold ladders/treads, beam bridges, crane-supported crossings.
- Persistent route guide lines stay subdued; selected legal routes become bright through the existing Moving Screen UI.
- Rebar, railings, parapets, fencing, and raised slab edges provide visual structure and selected knockback protection.

## Destructibles
- Rotten temporary stair: can collapse and remove one ascent route.
- Plywood partition: breaking it opens two alternate paths through an unfinished floor.
- Crate/lumber barricade: breaking it opens a shortcut.
- Temporary safety rail / slab lip: catches ordinary knockback while intact.

## Objective landmark
The final flag is at the Rooftop Crane Crown. Both player Dice and enemies use the standard Moving Screen flag ownership/drop/respawn rules.
